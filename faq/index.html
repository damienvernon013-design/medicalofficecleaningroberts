Medical Office Cleaning FAQs for Roberts Businesses

Common questions from practice managers in St. Croix County

Medical office cleaning questions — Roberts, WI

What's different about medical office cleaning compared to general commercial cleaning?

The main differences are disinfection protocol, product selection, and crew training. Clinical environments require EPA-registered disinfectants with documented dwell times (not just sprayed and wiped), room-specific protocols that prevent cross-contamination between exam rooms, and crews who understand HIPAA-aware handling of any patient materials encountered during cleaning. General commercial cleaning doesn't include these by default.

Do you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. A certificate of insurance is included with every written quote and available on request for renewal. We carry general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning in clinical environments. Details are on our Insured & Bonded page.

How do you price medical office cleaning?

Pricing depends on square footage, number of exam rooms or operatories, facility type, cleaning frequency, and any special protocol requirements. We don't publish a flat rate because it wouldn't be accurate for most facilities. We provide written quotes specific to each account. More on pricing →

Can you clean after hours or before the first patient?

Yes. Most of our medical accounts are cleaned in the evening after the last patient, or early morning before the first appointment. We confirm the schedule during the walkthrough and document it in the written scope of work.

Do you use EPA-registered disinfectants?

Yes. We use EPA List N disinfectants selected for the surface types in your facility. Dwell times are documented in the scope of work. We don't use general-purpose cleaners for clinical surfaces that require a registered disinfectant.

What happens if we need to change the cleaning schedule?

Call us at (866) 958-8773 during business hours. Schedule changes are confirmed in writing and reflected in the updated scope of work before the next visit. We don't make schedule changes by text or informal message.

Do you service dental practices specifically?

Yes. Dental operatory cleaning has specific requirements around instrument staging areas, dental unit waterline surfaces, and disinfection between patients. We have a dedicated service for dental practices. Dental & Specialty Practice Cleaning →

Do you serve communities outside Roberts?

Yes. We serve Roberts and the surrounding communities within a 25-mile radius: Hammond, Hudson, Somerset, New Richmond, River Falls, Baldwin, Woodville, and Star Prairie. See all service areas →

How quickly can you start?

We typically complete the walkthrough and written scope within the first week of inquiry. For accounts ready to start on a defined date, we can usually schedule the first clean within two weeks of the signed agreement. Rush starts may be possible — ask when you call.
