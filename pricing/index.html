Medical office cleaning cost in Roberts, WI

Medical office cleaning is priced differently from general commercial cleaning because the scope, products, and crew training requirements are more demanding. A square-foot price that applies to an office building does not translate to a clinical environment without adjustment for surface types, disinfection requirements, and turnaround time between patients.

We provide written quotes specific to your facility. Pricing varies based on:

  • Total cleanable square footage
  • Number of exam rooms, operatories, or procedure rooms
  • Space type (general medical, dental, urgent care, specialty practice)
  • Cleaning frequency (daily, multiple times per week, or weekly deep clean)
  • After-hours or weekend requirements
  • Special protocol requirements (terminal cleaning, bloodborne pathogen procedures)

What a written quote includes

Every quote we send is a written scope of work, not a number on a Post-it note. It specifies:

  • Which rooms and surfaces are included
  • Which products will be used and at what frequency
  • Dwell time requirements for disinfectants
  • Crew assignment and scheduled visit times
  • Price per visit and monthly total

A certificate of insurance comes with every written quote.
