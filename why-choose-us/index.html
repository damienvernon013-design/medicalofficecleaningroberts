Why Roberts Buildings Choose Us

22 years in clinical facility cleaning — the specifics that matter to practice managers

Why medical offices in Roberts choose Roberts Medical Office Cleaning

Practice managers evaluating cleaning contractors in St. Croix County generally encounter two options: general commercial cleaners who add healthcare to their service list, or specialty contractors who have actually built the training, protocols, and documentation to work in clinical environments. The difference isn't always visible in a brochure. It shows up during an infection control audit, after a complaint, or the first time a crew encounters something they weren't trained to handle.

22 years in clinical cleaning

We have been cleaning medical and dental facilities for 22 years. That's not a marketing claim — it's the length of time required to learn what general cleaners consistently get wrong in clinical settings: inadequate dwell times for disinfectants, cross-contamination between exam rooms from improper cloth-change protocol, surface-type mismatches between cleaning product and equipment, and crews that haven't been briefed on HIPAA-aware handling. We train for these specifically because we learned them specifically over two decades.

Documented protocols, not verbal agreements

Every Roberts Medical Office Cleaning account has a written scope of work on file before the first clean. That document specifies surfaces, products, frequencies, and dwell times. If a licensing board asks your practice manager for documentation of your cleaning protocol, you have a document. If a crew member is out and a substitute is needed, the substitute has a written protocol to follow.

Consistent crew assignment

We assign consistent crews to accounts, not rotating staff from a general pool. The crew who cleans your facility on Monday is the same crew the following Monday. They know your space layout, your equipment, your staff preferences, and your protocol. New faces on every visit is how errors happen in clinical settings.

Insurance and bonding — included with every quote

We carry general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning operations in clinical environments. A certificate of insurance is included with every written quote and available for renewal on request. Details on our coverage →

Honest pricing

We publish pricing on request, not as a teaser number that changes after the walkthrough. How our pricing works →

The direct comparison: Most practices in this region have no competing specialist cleaner in the local pack — the field is general commercial contractors. The bar isn't to beat another specialist; it's to document the difference between general and clinical-grade cleaning clearly enough that the practice manager doesn't have to take it on faith. We can do that.
