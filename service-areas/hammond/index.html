About Hammond

Hammond is the seat of Hammond Township, with a business district concentrated along STH-65 that includes a pharmacy, chiropractic clinic, and several general medical providers operating out of small professional buildings on the highway frontage.

The town sits at the intersection of STH-65 and County Road N, placing it on the direct route between Roberts and Baldwin — a corridor where healthcare traffic is steady given the absence of a hospital in either community.

Hammond's population of roughly 2,000 includes a higher-than-average proportion of residents over 65 compared to the broader St. Croix County average, creating baseline demand for routine medical and dental services that larger regional hospitals don't handle at the local level.
