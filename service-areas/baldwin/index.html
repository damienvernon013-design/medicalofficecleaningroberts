About Baldwin

Baldwin is located at the intersection of I-94 and STH-63, making it a significant transit point in eastern St. Croix County. Its medical infrastructure includes a small hospital-affiliated clinic on Eau Galle Avenue and a growing number of independent provider offices in the corridor between downtown and the interstate.

The I-94 interchange creates a sustained base of commercial and light industrial activity around Baldwin — trucking, distribution, and highway service — that drives occupational health and general medicine demand distinct from the residential population patterns in smaller nearby communities.

Baldwin has one of the higher concentrations of dental practices relative to population in St. Croix County, partly because its I-94 access makes it a practical stop for residents from Woodville, Roberts, and even Hammond who prefer a town with slightly more amenities for combined errands.
