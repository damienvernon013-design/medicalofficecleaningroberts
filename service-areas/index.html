Medical office cleaning near Roberts, WI

Roberts Medical Office Cleaning serves Roberts and the surrounding St. Croix and Pierce County communities within a 25-mile radius. Our crews run regular scheduled routes through the area, which means we can take on new accounts in any of these communities without disruption to existing schedules.

Every community listed below is within our standard service radius. Drive times from Roberts are confirmed before every new account is added — we don't list areas we can't service consistently.

Coverage notes

Hudson and New Richmond are the two largest medical facility markets in our coverage area. We run dedicated routes to both. River Falls has a University of Wisconsin campus with affiliated health services and a growing medical district along South Main Street. Hammond is the nearest community to Roberts and is serviced on the same route.

Baldwin, Woodville, and Star Prairie are smaller markets but each has medical and dental practices that are underserved by specialty clinical cleaners. We cover all three on a shared route that runs through the STH-65 corridor.

Somerset has its own established medical infrastructure, including a hospital campus on US-8 near the Apple River corridor, and is served on a dedicated route from Roberts.

Outside this area?

If your facility is outside the communities listed and within 30 miles of Roberts, call us at (866) 958-8773 and we'll tell you honestly whether we can service it. We don't commit to accounts we can't staff reliably.
