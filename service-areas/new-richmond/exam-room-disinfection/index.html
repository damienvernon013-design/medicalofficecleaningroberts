Exam room disinfection in New Richmond, WI

Exam room cleaning in New Richmond requires the same disinfection standards as any other clinical environment: EPA-registered products, documented dwell times, and room-to-room cross-contamination prevention. Being in a smaller community doesn't change the infection control requirement — it changes the scheduling and routing logistics, which we handle.

A typical account in New Richmond

An example of what we clean in New Richmond: a four-room internal medicine practice on South Knowles Avenue in New Richmond, adjacent to but separate from the Westfields campus, approximately 2,400 sq ft — cleaned Monday through Friday after the 4:30pm last appointment. The written scope documents surfaces, products, dwell times, and the cleaning sequence for each room. Crew assignment is consistent — the same people each visit.

What's included in exam room cleaning

  • Exam table surfaces and paper roll holders
  • All horizontal clinical surfaces, trays, and counters
  • Door hardware, light switches, and wall-mounted equipment
  • Sink fixtures and soap/sanitizer dispensers
  • Rolling stools and chair contact surfaces
  • Floor mopping with germicidal solution
