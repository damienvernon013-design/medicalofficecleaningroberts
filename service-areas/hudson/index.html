About Hudson

Hudson is the St. Croix County seat and the largest city in the county at approximately 14,000 residents. Its medical district is concentrated along Vine Street and the STH-35 corridor near the Lakeview Hospital campus, which anchors specialty referrals for the entire county.

The Lakeview Health complex on Vine Street draws specialist practice offices into the surrounding blocks — orthopedics, cardiology, oncology support, and physical therapy — creating a cluster of clinical facilities that all require specialized cleaning protocols distinct from general commercial cleaning.

Hudson's location on the St. Croix River directly across from Stillwater, MN creates cross-border patient traffic: Minnesota residents with Wisconsin-based providers and vice versa, which adds to the volume pressure on Hudson clinics and makes consistent daily cleaning schedules important rather than optional.
