Dental office cleaning in River Falls, WI

Dental practices in River Falls have the same operatory disinfection requirements as larger-market practices. The surface types, dwell time requirements for EPA-registered disinfectants, and instrument staging area protocols don't change with market size. Our scope is written the same way for a River Falls operatory as for a Hudson or New Richmond practice.

A typical account in River Falls

An example of what we clean in River Falls: a three-operatory general and pediatric dental practice near the University Avenue corridor in River Falls, with a patient mix that shifts significantly between the academic year and summer — daily cleaning September through May, scaled back to four evenings per week from June through August. The written scope specifies the operatory cleaning sequence, staging area protocol, and the products and dwell times for each surface type.

What's included in dental practice cleaning

  • Operatory surfaces: chair, delivery system, overhead light handles
  • Counter surfaces, cabinet hardware, and sink fixtures
  • Instrument staging areas (cleaned before instrument setup)
  • Waiting room and reception area disinfection
  • Restroom cleaning and high-touch surface disinfection
  • Floor mopping throughout
