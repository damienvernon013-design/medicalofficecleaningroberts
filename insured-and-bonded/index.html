Insured, Bonded and 22 Years Serving Roberts

Coverage details and documentation for clinical facility accounts

Insured and bonded medical office cleaning in Roberts, WI

Roberts Medical Office Cleaning has operated in the clinical facility cleaning vertical for 22 years. During that time we have maintained continuous general liability insurance coverage appropriate for commercial cleaning operations in medical and healthcare environments. A certificate of insurance is provided with every written quote and is available on request for any account at any time.

What our coverage includes

We carry general liability insurance appropriate for commercial cleaning operations in clinical environments. This covers bodily injury and property damage arising from our cleaning operations on your premises. All crew members working on Roberts Medical Office Cleaning accounts are covered under this policy.

We also maintain workers' compensation coverage. This means that in the event of an injury to one of our crew members while on your premises, the claim runs through our workers' compensation policy rather than yours. That distinction matters to practice managers responsible for their own liability exposure.

We are bonded. Our bond covers theft or dishonesty by employees on the job. Clinical environments often contain prescription samples, personal belongings, and petty cash. Bond coverage is a baseline credential for any cleaning contractor working in a medical facility.

Requesting documentation

If your practice, your landlord, or your licensing board requires a certificate of insurance naming your facility as an additional insured, we can accommodate that request. Provide the required certificate holder information when you contact us and we'll include it with your written quote.

22 years in business

A cleaning contractor's insurance history is easier to maintain when they have one. We have been operating continuously for 22 years. We have not had our coverage lapse, been dropped by a carrier, or had a claim history that created coverage problems. That's not a guarantee about the future — it's a factual description of our operating record.

Ready to request documentation? Call (866) 958-8773 during Mon–Fri 8:30am–5:00pm or submit a quote request and note that you need a certificate of insurance included.

No fabricated credentials

We do not publish policy numbers or specific coverage dollar amounts on this website. Those details change with policy renewals and the accurate figures are on the actual certificate, not a webpage. Any contractor who publishes policy numbers on a public website is either publishing outdated information or inviting verification of something they'd rather you not verify. We provide documentation when you ask for it, and it's current when you receive it.
