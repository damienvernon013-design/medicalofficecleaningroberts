Medical office cleaning requires a different standard

General cleaning contractors use the same products and protocols across offices, warehouses, and retail spaces. Clinical environments are different. Exam surfaces, dental operatories, waiting rooms shared by immunocompromised patients, and reception areas handling PHI all require specific disinfection procedures, dwell times, and crew training that general cleaning skips or approximates.

We work exclusively in medical and clinical settings. Our crews follow written cleaning protocols for each space type, use EPA List N disinfectants with verified dwell times, and are briefed on HIPAA-aware handling of any patient materials encountered during cleaning.
