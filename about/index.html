About Roberts Medical Office Cleaning

Clinical facility cleaning in St. Croix County, WI — 22 years in service

What we do and who we serve

Roberts Medical Office Cleaning provides scheduled cleaning and disinfection services to medical offices, dental practices, urgent care clinics, and specialty practices across Roberts, WI and the surrounding St. Croix and Pierce County region. We work exclusively in clinical and healthcare-adjacent environments — not general commercial accounts.

Our buyers are practice managers, clinic administrators, dental office managers, and facilities staff at multi-physician groups. These are professionals responsible for maintaining compliance with state licensing boards, infection control standards, and OSHA requirements. They need a cleaning contractor who understands that standard and can document it.

22 years in this vertical

We have been cleaning clinical and medical facilities for 22 years. That length of time in a specialized vertical isn't common. It means our crew training includes specific handling for exam rooms, dental operatories, waiting areas shared by vulnerable patient populations, and reception environments where PHI may be present. It means we have written protocols, not verbal ones. And it means that when a practice manager asks for our certificate of insurance or scope-of-work document, we have them ready.

We don't claim to be the largest cleaning operation in Wisconsin. We claim to be the right contractor for medical facilities in this region, and we can document every part of that claim.

Coverage area

We serve Roberts and the surrounding communities within a 25-mile radius, including Hammond, Hudson, Somerset, New Richmond, River Falls, Baldwin, Woodville, and Star Prairie. Regular scheduled routes run through St. Croix County and into Pierce County.
