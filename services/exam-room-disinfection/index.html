Protocol-based disinfection for exam rooms, procedure rooms, and clinical surfaces

Exam room cleaning in Roberts — what's included

Exam room disinfection in a working medical office isn't the same as cleaning a regular room. The surfaces involved — exam tables, wall-mounted blood pressure cuffs, otoscope handles, cabinet hardware, sink fixtures, rolling stools, and the dozen contact points a provider touches during a patient visit — each carry different contamination risk and may require different product selection.

Our exam room protocol addresses each of these surfaces specifically. We don't apply one product to all surfaces in the room and move on. The written scope for your account documents which product is used on which surface, the required contact time before wiping, and the order of cleaning to prevent recontamination.

What we disinfect

  • Exam tables and table paper rolls
  • All horizontal surfaces including counters, trays, and ledges
  • Door handles, light switch covers, and wall-mounted equipment
  • Sink fixtures and soap dispensers
  • Rolling stools and chair surfaces
  • Waste and sharps container exteriors
  • Floor mopping with germicidal solution

Cross-contamination prevention

One of the most common failures in clinical cleaning is carrying contamination from one exam room to the next via cleaning cloths and mop heads. Our protocol specifies fresh cloths per room and mop head changes between rooms. This isn't an optional enhancement — it's a baseline requirement for infection control compliance and it's written into every account's scope.

EPA-registered disinfectants

We use EPA List N disinfectants selected for the surface types and risk profile in your facility. Product selection and dwell times are documented in the written scope of work. More on EPA List N disinfectants and dwell times →

Frequency options

Daily cleaning after last patient is most common for multi-physician practices. Single-provider offices may find three-day or twice-weekly schedules sufficient depending on patient volume. We can also add a mid-day wipe-down protocol for high-volume practices. The schedule is confirmed in the written scope after the walkthrough.
