Our Medical Office Cleaning Services in Roberts

Exam room disinfection, dental practice cleaning, and waiting room maintenance

Medical office cleaning services — Roberts, WI

We provide three core service lines for medical and clinical facilities in Roberts and the surrounding St. Croix County region. Each service has a written protocol, EPA-registered product selection, and crew training specific to that space type. We do not offer a single "medical cleaning package" that applies identically to every account — the scope is written to your facility.

Exam Room & Clinical Space Disinfection

Systematic disinfection of exam tables and surfaces, door hardware, equipment contact points, and clinical counters. Protocol includes dwell time documentation and room-to-room cross-contamination prevention.

Full service details →

Dental & Specialty Practice Cleaning

Operatory cleaning between patients and end-of-day protocols, instrument staging area maintenance, and surface disinfection meeting dental infection control standards. Suitable for general dental, oral surgery, orthodontics, and specialty practices.

Full service details →

Waiting Room & Reception Cleaning

High-touch surface disinfection, seating, magazines and reading materials protocol, reception counter and glass, and public restrooms. Written protocol aligned with infection control standards for public-facing clinical space.

Full service details →

Service frequency options

Most medical office accounts run on a daily or five-day schedule, cleaning after the last patient of the day. Some smaller single-provider practices clean three days per week. Dental practices often request a cleaning between morning and afternoon sessions in addition to end-of-day cleaning. We'll document the schedule in the written scope of work after the walkthrough.
