What to have ready when you call

We can give you an accurate quote faster if you have a few details on hand:

  • Total square footage of cleanable space
  • Number of exam rooms, operatories, or procedure rooms
  • Preferred cleaning frequency (daily, weekly, or after-hours)
  • Any compliance requirements specific to your practice type (dental, urgent care, specialty)
  • Preferred start date

If you don't have all of that, call anyway. We'll walk through the questions together.

Prefer to write? Use our quote request form and we'll respond by the next business day with a written quote and a certificate of insurance.
